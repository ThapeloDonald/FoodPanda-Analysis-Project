### Project Overview
This project analyses Foodpanda customer order data to uncover insights related to customer behaviour, revenue, and delivery performance. The goal is to help the business make data-driven decisions that improve operational performance and customer satisfaction.

### Data Source
Foodpanda analysis dataset: The dataset used for this analysis is a "Foodpanda_analysis_dataset.csv" sourced from Kaggle.com

### Business Problem
Foodpanda operates in a competitive food delivery market where delivery performance directly impact revenue.

#### The company needs to understand:
 -	Why customers churn.
 -	Which cities, restaurants, and products drive the most revenue.
 -	How delivery delays affect customer satisfaction.
 -	Most used customers payment methods.
 
Without these insights, Foodpanda risks losing customers, misallocating marketing spend and underperforming operationally.

### Objectives
 -	Analyse customer demographics and order behaviour.
 -	Identify top-performing restaurants and dishes.
 -	Evaluate delivery performance and customer ratings.
 -	Identify factors related to customer churn.
 -	Provide actionable business recommendations.
 
### Dataset
 -	6000 records.
 -	Key attributes:
    -  Customer demographics (Age Group, Gender, City).
    -  Order details (Restaurant, Dish, Category, Quantity, Price).
    -  Customer behaviour (Order Frequency, Loyalty Points, Churn Status).
    -  Delivery performance (Delivered, Delayed, Cancelled).
    -  Payment methods (Card, Cash, Wallet).
    -  Customer ratings (1-5).
    
### Methodology
 -	Data cleaning and validation in Excel
 -	Exploratory Data Analysis using Pivot Tables
 -	KPI analysis (Total Revenue, Total Orders, Average Order Value, Churn Rate, Average Customer Rating).
 -	Dashboard creation for business insights.
 
### Tools and Skills Used
 -	Microsoft Excel.
 -	Pivot Tables & Charts.
 -	Data Cleaning & EDA techniques.
 -	Data Visualisation.
 
### Key Findings and Insights
 -	Teenage customers generate the highest order volume.
 -	Senior customers generate more revenue. 
 -	Three restaurants contribute more to the revenue.
 -	Continental dishes generate more revenue while Italian dishes generate more orders.
 -	Delayed orders strongly correlate with higher churn while cancelled orders strongly correlate with low ratings.
 -	The City Multan generates more revenue and orders.
 -	Cash is the most payment method used by customers.
 
### Business Recommendations
 -	Improve delivery efficiency in high-volume cities.
 -	Promote high-performing restaurants and dishes.
 -	Encourage digital payments through incentives.
 -	Monitor delivery delays as an early churn indicator.
 
### Future Improvements
 -	Churn prediction using machine learning.
 -	RFM customer segmentation.
 -	Migration to Power BI for advanced dashboards.
 -	Real-time operational monitoring.
 
### Outcome
This analysis provides actionable insights that support revenue growth and operational optimisation for Foodpanda.
